Two linked paths

From deployment targets to training data assets.

JoyAI-Sim organizes simulation as a toolchain, not just a renderer. One path evaluates robot policies before costly hardware trials; the other converts human behavior data into robot-consumable samples.

Robot → Simulation → Human

Efficient model evaluation

Real robot tasks define the deployment goal. Digital twins reproduce scene layout, robot embodiment, sensing, control, and success predicates so policies can be screened at scale. Human embodied feedback then checks whether feasible trajectories also feel natural.

  • Parallel simulation trials with repeatable resets
  • Controlled perturbations over layout, objects, language, and lighting
  • Human feedback for global trajectory naturalness
Human → Simulation → Robot

Data value-added service

Ego-centric human videos are lifted into simulation-ready demonstrations through hand-motion recovery, scene reconstruction, and digital-twin authoring. Simulation retargets feasible motion to robots and expands it into robot-view trajectories.

  • Human video becomes a metric, executable task instance
  • Physics filters impossible contacts, collisions, and unreachable motion
  • Randomized rendering creates diverse robot training observations

Path 1

Robot → Simulation: make hardware tasks reproducible.

The real robot benchmark anchors task semantics, object-target mappings, reset states, and final deployment validation. JoyAI-Sim reconstructs those conditions as a digital twin for scalable diagnostic evaluation.

Simulation as a diagnostic layer

Once the physical scene and robot interaction loop are aligned, the same task can be replayed under controlled changes in robot state, object layout, object instance, background, instruction, and illumination. Failures become attributable instead of being collapsed into one success rate.

Simulation → Human

Physics feasibility is not enough. Trajectories also need embodied plausibility.

JoyAI-Sim synthesizes trajectories with FSM+IK, IKFlow, and reinforcement learning. Human operators then simulate the robot end-effector motion in hand space, surfacing awkward approach paths, missing anticipatory adjustments, and phase transitions that look valid but feel wrong.

FSM + IK Bootstraps seed data without demonstrations.
IKFlow Expands one feasible trajectory into diverse smooth rollouts.
RL generation Explores robust strategies for contact-rich and long-horizon tasks.

Path 2

Human → Simulation → Robot: convert human behavior into robot training resources.

First-person human demonstrations are not directly executable by robots. JoyAI-Sim routes them through simulation, where motion, scene, and contacts can be grounded, filtered, randomized, and rendered from the robot point of view.

01

Recover human motion

Extract wrist trajectory, fingertip contact, and grasp or release events from ego-centric video.

02

Author a digital twin

Reconstruct objects, containers, support surfaces, camera geometry, and task-relevant assets.

03

Retarget and filter

Enforce reachability, joint limits, collision constraints, contact timing, and physical consistency.

04

Render robot data

Output robot-view videos, states, actions, trajectories, and task labels for downstream learning.

Simulation-side data expansion

From the same human seed, the simulator can vary object positions, container layouts, object combinations, materials, lighting, background, and camera appearance while preserving task feasibility. The result is a scalable data asset: cheap human video becomes verified robot-centered training data.
